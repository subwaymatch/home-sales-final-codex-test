Reading the numbers. The valid price rule is $10,000–$5,000,000 inclusive, except Q2 and Q3's all-row comparisons. Days on market is contract date minus list date and excludes missing dates. Year and month use sale date. City means city plus state; campus area means the university field. Rows are keyed by listing ID, never address. Medians are used unless a question asks for means. Tax rows require a positive annual tax below sale price. Distance is straight-line miles. Source: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026), University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies.
